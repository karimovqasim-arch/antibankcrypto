About AntiBank Crypto

AntiBank Crypto (ABCR) is an experimental blockchain token built on BNB Smart Chain.

Its smart contract includes a burn-and-reward mechanism. Users may burn tokens to establish reward eligibility for subsequent time-based epochs, subject to the conditions and limits defined in the contract.

Burning tokens does not guarantee profits, token value, or future rewards.
