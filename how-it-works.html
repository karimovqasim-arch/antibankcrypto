GETTING STARTED

How ABCR works.

A step-by-step guide to connecting a wallet, understanding burn participation and claiming eligible rewards.

01 / Connect your wallet

  1. Open the ABCR App.
  2. On a mobile browser, enable Desktop site in the browser menu before attempting WalletConnect. This is a current website limitation, not a guarantee that all wallets will connect.
  3. Select Connect Wallet and authorize the connection in your wallet.
  4. Confirm that the selected network is BNB Smart Chain (chain ID 56).
  5. Check the displayed wallet address and ABCR balance.
Never share your seed phrase or private key. ABCR does not need either to connect your wallet.

02 / Understand the burn

Enter the amount of ABCR you intend to burn. The website calls burnForShares(amount) after wallet confirmation. The burn is irreversible and requires BNB for gas.

Only burn tokens you are prepared to lose permanently. Participation accounting and reward eligibility are controlled by the deployed smart contract.

03 / Understand epochs

ABCR uses 30-minute reward epochs. The stated reward pool is 100 ABCR for each epoch, subject to the smart contract's rules and lifetime limits.

Important: 100 ABCR is the epoch pool, not a guaranteed reward for each wallet. The actual amount depends on eligibility and the contract's allocation rules.

04 / Claim eligible rewards

  1. Check Current epoch and Next claim epoch on the app.
  2. Enter a maximum epoch count between 1 and 24.
  3. Press Claim Rewards and review the wallet confirmation.
  4. Wait for the transaction to confirm and check the result on BscScan.
Entering 1 in the claim field means “process up to one epoch,” not “receive 1 ABCR.”

06 / Frequently asked questions

Does burning 1 ABCR guarantee 100 ABCR?

No. The 100 ABCR figure describes an epoch reward pool. Eligibility and payouts depend on the deployed contract.

Can I undo a burn?

No. A confirmed burn is irreversible.

Why do I need BNB?

BNB is used to pay BNB Smart Chain network transaction fees.

Why does WalletConnect ask me to open Trust Wallet repeatedly?

Mobile wallet handoff may be unreliable in the current version. Use the browser's Desktop Site mode as the current workaround, and do not repeatedly approve unexpected prompts.
